# 100 Roblox game ideas

Generated from `public/js/ideas.js`. Each idea: the core loop, the twist that makes it different, and how it makes Robux. Load any of them in Pitcher from the Ideas tab and press "Pitch it for me".

## Steal-a (22)

Expert in the crowd: Pip, Steal-a expert.

1. **Throw an Egg**: Steal eggs from other bases and throw them over the walls into your nest; eggs in your nest earn cash every second. *Twist:* nothing is carried home, every steal is an aimed throw and a miss cracks the egg, so stealing becomes a skill shot. *Money:* nest-lock game pass, egg skins, a trajectory trail that is cosmetic only.
2. **Kick an Egg**: Kick eggs like footballs across the map into your base, where they earn cash per second. *Twist:* eggs bounce off walls and other bases, defenders can block shots like goalkeepers, so bases become little football pitches. *Money:* boot cosmetics, goal-net base lock, developer product for an instant egg.
3. **Roll an Egg**: Steal giant eggs from bases on a hill and roll them down to your base at the bottom. *Twist:* the map is a slope, eggs pick up speed and can flatten other players, and uphill bases are harder to rob but slower to fill. *Money:* lock passes, egg skins, a sticky-glove product to stop a runaway egg.
4. **Launch an Egg**: Steal eggs and fire them home from a catapult you upgrade in your base. *Twist:* other players can shoot your eggs out of the sky with their own catapults, so every steal is an artillery duel. *Money:* catapult skins, extra catapult slot pass, shield products.
5. **Steal a Dragon Egg**: Steal dragon eggs that hatch in your base on a timer; hatched dragons earn far more cash. *Twist:* eggs can be stolen only until they hatch, so the last minute before hatching is a siege. *Money:* hatch-speed pass, nest lock, limited event dragons.
6. **Steal a Snowball**: Steal snowballs from other bases and roll them home; they grow bigger the further you roll. *Twist:* bigger snowballs earn more but make you slower and easier to catch, so greed is the risk. *Money:* winter skins, lock pass, a sled product for a fast getaway.
7. **Carry a Cake**: Steal layered cakes from bakeries and carry them to your bakery to earn cash per second. *Twist:* carrying a tall cake makes you wobble, and every hit knocks a layer off for someone else to grab. *Money:* cake toppers, a tray pass that steadies one layer, bakery lock.
8. **Steal a Meteor**: Meteors crash into random spots; carry them to your base before they cool to earn cash. *Twist:* hot meteors burn the carrier, so you pass them between teammates like hot potatoes. *Money:* heat-glove cosmetics, landing radar pass, lock refresh products.
9. **Bowl an Egg**: Steal eggs and bowl them through other bases to knock defenders over, then collect them in your base. *Twist:* a strike stuns every defender in a base for five seconds, so one good roll opens a raid. *Money:* bowling-egg skins, lane-guide cosmetic, base lock.
10. **Hook an Egg**: Steal eggs from other islands by casting fishing rods and reeling them into your base. *Twist:* lines can be cut by defenders and tangled by other fishers, so stealing is a tug of war. *Money:* rod skins, extra line pass, bait products for rarer eggs.
11. **Steal a Cloud**: Bases are floating clouds; jump across and carry cloud creatures back to earn cash. *Twist:* clouds drift, so bases move closer and further over time and routes change every minute. *Money:* cloud skins, drift-anchor pass, cloud lock.
12. **Balloon Steal**: Tie stolen eggs to balloons and let them float back to your base. *Twist:* balloons can be popped by anyone with a dart, so you escort your loot through the sky. *Money:* balloon skins, extra balloon slot, dart cosmetics.
13. **Magnet Heist**: Steal metal eggs from bases using magnets you upgrade. *Twist:* magnets have polarity, so the right defence can push a thief's magnet away and fling their loot. *Money:* magnet skins, strength upgrades capped for fairness, base lock.
14. **Steal a Train Car**: Every base is a train car on a looping track; jump between moving trains to steal cargo. *Twist:* stations stop the trains for ten seconds, which is when the big raids happen. *Money:* car skins, station-pass for a longer stop at your car, lock.
15. **Swap an Egg**: Instead of stealing, sneak into bases and swap their best egg for a fake. *Twist:* decoys look identical until someone tries to sell them, so bluffing and checking become the game. *Money:* decoy skins, scanner pass that reveals one fake per hour, lock.
16. **Sneak an Egg**: Steal eggs past guard cones in each base using stealth. *Twist:* owners place guards and cameras, so defending is a puzzle you design and stealing is a puzzle you solve. *Money:* guard skins, extra guard slot, cosmetic disguises.
17. **Steal a Ghost**: Catch ghosts drifting out of other bases with a lantern and lead them home to haunt-earn cash. *Twist:* ghosts phase through walls and drift back unless the lantern stays lit, so you keep an eye on fuel. *Money:* lantern skins, fuel products, spooky lock.
18. **Dig an Egg**: Steal eggs buried under other bases by digging tunnels and pulling them down into your vault. *Twist:* owners can flood tunnels or collapse them, so stealing happens underground in real time. *Money:* shovel skins, tunnel-map pass, drill products.
19. **Juggle Eggs**: Steal several eggs at once by juggling them all the way home. *Twist:* every hit you take drops one egg, so greedy jugglers lose the most. *Money:* juggling-trail cosmetics, extra-hand pass capped at five eggs, lock.
20. **Steal a Bee**: Steal bees from other hives; bees make honey cash in your hive. *Twist:* stolen bees fly home unless you also steal the queen, so the queen raid is the big play. *Money:* hive skins, queen-guard pass, flower boost products.
21. **Slingshot Heist**: Slingshot yourself into other bases, grab an egg and slingshot back. *Twist:* landing is physics, so misses bounce you into the moat and bases are built around the landing zones. *Money:* slingshot skins, aim-preview cosmetic, lock.
22. **Tow a Car**: Steal cars from other garages by hooking them to your tow truck and dragging them home. *Twist:* cars swing on the tow rope and can be cut loose, so every steal is a getaway chase. *Money:* truck skins, chain upgrades, garage lock.
